A little about me

Stories can make
the unfamiliar feel closer.

I’m Maria, and I create experiences that inspire new perspectives and build understanding.

I was raised in Atlanta by immigrant parents from rural Mexico during a time of shifting socio-political changes, so I developed an awareness of identity early on. This curiosity led me to join grassroots organizations and contribute to collaborative projects that challenged me to see the world differently and share those insights with others.

During that time, I also discovered video production through a high school elective, and it has remained my chosen storytelling medium ever since. Over the next three to five years, my mission is to strengthen my creative framework and production skills to develop and advocate for diverse stories in film, television, and audiovisual media.
